# Allergies & Intolerances

## Drug Allergies
- **Penicillin** — reaction: hives and facial swelling (documented 1999). Anaphylaxis not confirmed.
- **Sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim** — reaction: rash (documented 2015).

## Intolerances
- **Metformin GI intolerance** (historical) — diarrhea; switched to SGLT2 inhibitor.
- **Enalapril cough** — dose-related; changed to losartan.

## Notes
- No allergy to SGLT2 inhibitors, ARBs, or beta-blockers.
- Carry allergy band; avoid penicillins and sulfa drugs.
