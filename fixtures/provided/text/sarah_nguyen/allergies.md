# Allergies & Intolerances

## Drug Allergies
- **None known (NKDA)** — documented 2023.

## Intolerances
- **Caffeine (lifestyle)** — increases anxiety and panic symptoms; substantially reduced intake.

## Notes
- No drug allergies. No allergies to SSRIs or SNRIs.
- Advise avoidance of high-dose caffeine and stimulants which can provoke panic.
