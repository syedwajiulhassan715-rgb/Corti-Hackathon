# Cancer Summary (NSCLC)

## Diagnosis
- **Non-small cell lung cancer (adenocarcinoma), stage IIIA (T2aN2M0)** — ICD-10 C34.31
- Date of diagnosis: 2026-06-22
- Laterality: Right upper lobe (RUL)

## Presentation / Workup
- Presented 2026-05 with persistent cough and unintentional weight loss (~4 kg over 2 months).

- Biopsy (EBUS-TBNA): adenocarcinoma, poorly differentiated.
- PET-CT: FDG-avid RUL mass + right hilar/subcarinal lymph nodes; no distant metastases.
- Brain MRI: negative.
- Clinical stage: IIIA (T2aN2M0).

## Molecular Profile

- ALK: negative
- ROS1: negative
- PD-L1: 45%

## Treatment Plan

  - Carboplatin + paclitaxel weekly
  - Radiation therapy to primary + involved mediastinum (60 Gy / 30 fractions, completed 08-28)
- **Phase 2 — Consolidation immunotherapy** (planned after CRT):
  - Durvalumab consolidation for 12 months given PD-L1 ≥1% status (planned; awaiting clearance post-CRT)
- ECOG performance status at baseline: 1

## On-Treatment Monitoring
- Weekly CBC during CRT; antiemetic support; neutropenia support with pegfilgrastim.
- Radiation pneumonitis and esophagitis precautions (radiation therapist will coordinate).
- Assess response with restaging CT ~1 month after completion.

## Toxicity / Adverse Events (so far)
- Mild esophagitis (grade 1) and fatigue during CRT — manageable.
- Mild anemia and borderline ANC at nadir — supported by pegfilgrastim.
- No febrile neutropenia episodes.

## Survivorship / Follow-up Plan
- Restaging scans at ~4–6 weeks post-radiation.
- Ongoing symptom management and nutrition support (dietitian involved).
- Smoking cessation maintained (quit at diagnosis).
- Annual surveillance CT thereafter.
