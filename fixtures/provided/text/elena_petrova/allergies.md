# Allergies & Intolerances

## Drug Allergies
- **Penicillin** — reaction: rash and mild urticaria (documented 1980). Anaphylaxis not confirmed.
- **Sulfa drugs** — reaction: rash (documented 2005).

## Intolerances
- **Codeine** — drowsiness/constipation.

## Notes
- No allergy to macrolides, fluoroquinolones, or doxycycline.
- Penicillin/sulfa allergy important for antibiotic selection.
