# Clinical truth
Doctor owns this file. Nobody else edits it. Values here become engines/rules/ verbatim.

## 1 The three patients
Room, condition, arc across the demo, what each is waiting on.
One improving, one deteriorating, one stable but stalled.

## 2 Patient State rules
Condition to level. Include correlations: which spoken observations, over what window,
by how many different speakers, produce yellow or red.

## 3 Coordination rules
Which stage delays are yellow, which are red, and the time thresholds.

## 4 Task table
What, why, urgency band, intended recipient, due window. 10-15 rows.

## 5 Scoring weights
Urgency, wait, dependency, fit. Four numbers with reasoning.

## 6 Interruptibility
Which activities may be interrupted, which may not.

## 7 Ward-round ladder
Confirm or amend the six-step order.

## 8 Scripts
Realistic clinical language. Must contain: one planted conflict, one deliberately
unaddressed question, one nurse audibly mid-encounter, one audible decline of an offer.

## 9 Answer key
Expected level per room over time, expected tasks, expected queue order. Becomes the eval.
